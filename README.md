# CivicPulse AI 🏙️
### Bridging the Gap Between Citizens and Government Departments

CivicPulse AI is an AI-assisted civic grievance management platform designed to make reporting public infrastructure and municipal issues simpler, more transparent, and easier to track.

The platform helps citizens describe civic problems, identify the relevant department, prepare structured complaints, and follow their progress. A dedicated department interface supports complaint review, progress updates, delay explanations, and resolution communication.

## 🚩 The Problem

Citizens frequently encounter civic issues such as potholes, overflowing garbage bins, water supply disruptions, damaged streetlights, and drainage problems.

However, reporting these issues can be difficult because:

- Citizens may not know which department is responsible.
- Complaint submission processes can be confusing.
- Tracking the status of a complaint may be difficult.
- Citizens may not receive clear explanations for delays.
- Communication between citizens and responsible departments can lack transparency.

These challenges can reduce public trust and make civic issue resolution more difficult.

## 💡 Our Solution

CivicPulse AI aims to simplify the civic grievance journey through a unified, citizen-friendly interface.

Instead of requiring citizens to understand complex departmental structures, the platform guides them through complaint reporting and helps connect each issue with the likely responsible department.

The platform also introduces a department-facing workflow where authorized staff in a future integrated deployment can review complaints, record progress, and communicate status updates.

**Our core idea:** Citizens should be able to focus on describing the problem rather than figuring out the entire administrative process.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Citizen Portal
- A simplified interface for reporting civic problems.
- Structured complaint information for easier review.
- A citizen-oriented experience designed to reduce reporting friction.

### 2. AI-Assisted Complaint Routing
- Support for classifying complaints by issue type.
- Assistance in identifying the likely responsible department.
- Potential prioritization based on urgency and reported impact.

*The availability and accuracy of AI-based classification depend on the integrations implemented in the current build.*

### 3. Department Portal
- A dedicated interface for reviewing reported issues.
- A structured workflow for recording complaint progress.
- Support for communicating status changes and resolution information.
